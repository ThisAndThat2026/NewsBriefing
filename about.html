What this is

A short daily news briefing for the state, counties and countries you choose. It is a free, non-commercial hobby project. It has no ads, no accounts and no paywall.

How stories are chosen

An AI assistant (Claude, made by Anthropic) searches for news from the last 24 hours and ranks it by consequence: how many people a story affects, how long it lasts, and whether it changes something you need to do. Anything that changes your plans in the next week, such as a road closure, a storm or a deadline, is labeled Action or listed under “Coming up.” If a place has no significant news, the briefing says “No major updates.” It never fills the gap with made-up stories.

AI can make mistakes. Every headline links to the original article, so please check anything that matters to you there.

What we show from publishers

Only a headline, a 1–2 sentence summary written in our own words, the publisher's name, the date, and a link to the original article. We never republish articles or photos, and we don't get around paywalls.

Privacy

Your chosen places are stored only in your browser on this device, using “localStorage.” They also appear in the page address after the “#” sign, which browsers don't send to the server. That's what makes the “Share link” button work. We don't use accounts, cookies, analytics or trackers. The server only sees an ordinary request for the same daily files that every visitor gets.

To erase your settings, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Corrections, takedowns and publisher opt-outs

If you're a publisher or rights holder and want a summary corrected or removed, or want your site excluded entirely, email [contact email — set before launch] with the link in question. We aim to act within 2 business days. Excluded sites stay excluded from future briefings.
